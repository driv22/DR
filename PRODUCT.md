## Product Principles

1. **Checkable over impressive.** Every number on the page traces to the résumé. When a
   claim can't be sourced, the page names the specific thing instead of rounding up.
2. **The operator years are the differentiator.** The pre-Microsoft career is not filler
   before the good part — it is the reason the current title means something.
3. **Under a minute.** A senior visitor gets scope, proof, and a way to reach him without
   scrolling. Everything else rewards the reader who stays.
4. **Financial services is the frame, not a tag.** Regulated-industry constraints are the
   context for every capability claim.
5. **Ages without edits.** Prefer "since 2000" to a hard year count; prefer named
   credentials to a total that drifts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Read on laptops and phones in bright rooms by an audience skewing older than the tech
median. WCAG AA contrast is a floor, not a target; keyboard navigation and visible focus
must work; `prefers-reduced-motion` must be honored.
